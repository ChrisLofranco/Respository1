How to Maintain Your Driveway

A few simple habits keep asphalt smooth, safe, and lasting longer. Here's what we tell our own customers.

De-icing

Use sand or a driveway-safe de-icer for grip. Go easy on harsh rock salt, and don't pile it in one place — spread it thin and even.

Shoveling technique

Choose a plastic-edged shovel over a steel one so you don't scrape or gouge the surface. Push snow off rather than chipping at packed ice.

Drainage

Keep water flowing off and away from the driveway and its edges. Standing water and pooling are the fastest way to break asphalt down over time.

Cracks

Address small cracks early, before water works in and freeze-thaw cycles widen them. A minor fill now saves a bigger repair later.

Heavy loads

Avoid parking heavy vehicles, trailers, or dropping dumpsters and equipment in the same spot — especially on hot days when asphalt is softer and dents more easily.

Spills

Clean up gasoline, oil, and solvents promptly. They soften and eat into asphalt. A quick rinse and a bit of degreaser goes a long way.
