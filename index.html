Free resource

How to Maintain Your Driveway

Simple, practical tips to keep your asphalt in good shape for years. Read them here or download the printable guide.

Winter de-icing

Reach for sand or a driveway-safe de-icer for traction. Go easy on harsh salt and avoid piling it in one spot.

Shoveling technique

Use a plastic-edged shovel, not steel, so you don't gouge the surface. Push snow off — don't chip at ice.

Drainage

Keep water moving off and away from the edges. Standing water and pooling are what break asphalt down over time.

Cracks

Deal with small cracks early before water gets in and freeze-thaw widens them. Small fixes beat big repairs.

Heavy loads

Avoid parking heavy vehicles or dropping dumpsters/equipment in the same spot, especially on hot days when asphalt softens.

Spills

Clean up gas, oil, and solvents quickly — they soften and eat into asphalt. A quick rinse goes a long way.
